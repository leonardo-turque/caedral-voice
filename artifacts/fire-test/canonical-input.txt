The old lighthouse keeper climbed the spiral stairs each evening, counting the worn steps out of habit rather than need. At the top, he polished the great lamp, checked the wick, and watched the first stars appear over the water. Fishing boats returned to the harbor in a slow procession, their lights trembling on the swells. He remembered when the fleet had been larger, when children had run along the pier to meet their fathers, when the whole town had smelled of salt and tar and fresh bread. The tide rose quietly beneath the old wooden pier.
